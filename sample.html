Snorlax
Normal

Snorlax is a Normal-type Pokémon. It is known for its enormous size, gentle demeanor, and endless appetite. It sleeps most of the time and only wakes up to eat.

  • Height: 2.1m (6′11″)
  • Weight: 460.0kg (1014.1 lbs)
  • Abilities: Immunity, Thick Fat
  • Body Slam - May paralyze the opponent
  • Rest - Fully restores HP and heals status
  • Snore - Can be used while asleep
  • Heavy Slam - Deals more damage based on weight difference
