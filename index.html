Snorlax (カビゴン, Kabigon?)

Snorlax is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation I. It is known as the "Sleeping Pokémon" and is famous for its large size and tendency to sleep in inconvenient places.

SNORLAX MAIN SKILL SET

Body Slam

Effects

Body Slam deals damage and has a 30% chance of paralyzing the target.

Electric type Pokémon, those with the ability Limber or those behind a Substitute cannot be paralyzed.

If the target has used Minimize, Body Slam ignores accuracy and deals double damage.

Changes

In Generations 1-5, the Minimize effect did not occur.

Z-Move Effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Body Slam turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 160.

Fire Punch

Fire Punch deals damage and has a 10% chance of burning the target.

Fire type Pokémon, those with the ability Flash Fire or those behind a Substitute cannot be burned.

If the target has used Minimize, Fire Punch ignores accuracy and deals double damage.

Changes

In Generations 1-3, Fire Punch is categorized as Special.

Z-Move Effects

When a Pokémon is holding Firium Z and uses its Z-Power, Fire Punch turns into Inferno Overdrive and has base power 140.

Earthquake

Earthquake deals damage and hits all adjacent Pokémon.

In battle, it is known for its high base power and ability to hit multiple targets.

If the target has used Minimize, Earthquake ignores accuracy and deals double damage.

Changes

In Generations 1-3, Earthquake is categorized as Special.

Z-Move Effects

When a Pokémon is holding Firium Z and uses its Z-Power, Earthquake turns into Tectonic Rage and has base power 200.
