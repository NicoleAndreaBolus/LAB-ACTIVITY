Pokédex Entry

Snorlax is a Normal-type Pokémon. It is known for its enormous size, gentle demeanor, and endless appetite. It sleeps most of the time and only wakes up to eat.

Battle Stats

Pokédex Flavor Text

"Very lazy. Just eats and sleeps. As its rotund bulk builds, it becomes steadily more slothful." – Pokémon Red/Blue

Did You Know?

Snorlax was the first Pokémon to be shown sleeping in the middle of a route, blocking the player's path until awakened using a Poké Flute!
